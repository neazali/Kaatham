Spiti Circuit

12 Days · 11 Nights  ·  Kerala to Kerala

₹21,000 per person

Cross five mountain passes into the cold desert of Spiti — monasteries older than most nations, villages at 15,000 feet, and a lake that turns the sky inside out at Chandratal. Small group, one trip leader, zero guesswork.

About This Trip

Route

Shimla → Sangla → Chitkul → Kalpa → Tabo → Nako → Kaza → Hikkim → Komic → Chandratal Lake → Manali

Highlights

  • Key Monastery & the villages of Hikkim and Komic — the world's highest motorable post office
  • Chandratal Lake camping under an open sky
  • Crossing Kunzum and Rohtang passes
  • Homestays in Kalpa and Tabo

Departure

Sep 21 – Oct 02  ·  Kerala to Kerala  ·  ₹21,000 per head

Inclusions

  • Stay: hotels, homestays & camping as per itinerary
  • All meals during the trip
  • Travel by tempo traveller / SUV as needed
  • Trip leader & local guide support
  • Permits for restricted areas

Exclusions

  • Personal expenses & shopping
  • Travel insurance
  • Anything not mentioned under inclusions
